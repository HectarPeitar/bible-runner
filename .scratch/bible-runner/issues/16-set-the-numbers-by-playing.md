# 16: Set the numbers by playing

Type: build
Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 18

**What to build:** the spec's one declared open end — the tuning numbers. Play the runner until the Rules feel right, then set them in the one module that holds them: Row spacing, approach time at 1×, the Speed ramp and ceiling, the Stumble slowdown and its duration, how far back a Stumble pushes the Runner, and the width of a Lane. This is the ticket that answers the question the whole effort exists to answer: is this genuinely fun for sixty seconds?

Record what felt wrong, in this ticket's answer: Distractors that read as unfair, whether Lives or a timer punishes better, whether a miss teaches anything or only feels bad, and whether the Speed curve rewards mastery or just makes a good Run end sooner.

- [ ] Every tuning number lives in the Rules module and nothing else reads it
- [ ] The tuned values are recorded here with the reason for each, including the per-Locale cadence question left open by ADR-0002
- [ ] The chosen numbers are written down with the reason they were chosen
- [ ] The play findings are recorded in this ticket: distractors, Lives versus a timer, whether a miss teaches
- [ ] A stated verdict on whether the loop is fun, which is the effort's real acceptance bar
- [ ] Anything the numbers disproved in the spec is raised as a change to the spec, not silently deviated from
