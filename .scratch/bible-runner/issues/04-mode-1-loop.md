# Pin the Book Order run loop

Type: grilling
Status: claimed
Blocked by:

## Question

Pin the loop precisely enough to build, in words rather than code.

**Settled by the operator** — Book Order has two **routes**, both reachable from the same mode, the same shell and the same kind of scoreboard:

- **Section route** — loops a single section (Gospels, Pauline Epistles), speed climbing each lap, so a player who knows the books cold still has somewhere to go.
- **Canon route** — runs the whole canon, and can **start at any point**: start at the Gospels, start at the Law, run whatever stretch the player is drilling.

Still to pin:

- **Where the canon route ends.** Revelation is a natural finish. Wrapping back to Genesis makes it endless, like a section lap. Decide, and say what a player who starts at Isaiah is dooming themselves to — Isaiah through Revelation and done, or Isaiah, Revelation, Genesis, onward.
- **Start point granularity.** Any of the 66 books, or only the ten section boundaries? A section-grouped picker is easier to browse; "start at Malachi" needs book granularity.
- **Gate geometry**: how many gates are visible ahead, how many lanes, what the player does each beat.
- **The choice each beat**: is exactly one correct next book always available? What if the correct gate is off-screen or crowded out?
- **Lives and how a run ends**: how many lives, does a wrong gate cost a life and continue or end the run, and does a section route end on lives, on laps, or never?
- **After a miss**: what the player is shown — the correct book, its neighbours in the order, its alias, and whether they will meet it again.
- **Speed**: what drives the ramp (elapsed time, gates passed, laps completed), whether the player can also choose a starting difficulty, and whether there is a ceiling.
- **Score**: what counts — books passed, laps, speed — and what a penalty is worth.
- **Distractor policy**: near misses from the same section first, then same testament, never repeating a gate within a run, never showing the answer twice.
